# Educational content standards

- Verify every mathematical answer and its stated conditions.
- Every multiple-choice question must have exactly one correct answer.
- Do not include mathematically equivalent distractors.
- Give every question a valid chapter and non-empty, relevant `topicTags`.
- Mark repeating decimals clearly, and label rounded or approximate values as approximate.
- Use clear, concise wording that is unambiguous in a competitive-exam setting.
- Make distractors plausible but demonstrably incorrect.
- Write formulas with notation and grouping that make their mathematical meaning unambiguous.
